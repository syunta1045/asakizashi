# App Review Response - 2026-06-05

## English

Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for reviewing 朝しるべ and for the continued guidance.

In this build, we made a product-level revision so the current experience is positioned and implemented as a morning self-care and reflection journal.

The main user flows are:

- Morning memo: a short daily self-care note with practical actions.
- Today's pace: morning/day/evening guidance for choosing a realistic pace.
- Evening reflection: mood and short note logging.
- Monthly calendar: a simple view for planning and reviewing self-care habits.
- Relationship memo: notes for choosing words and distance with important people.
- Premium: theme-based hints, monthly calendar, and reflection support through App Store in-app purchase.

We also updated the app metadata, promotional text, screenshots, support pages, and in-app labels so they consistently describe the app as a self-care and reflection journal.

The app can be fully reviewed without sign-in after onboarding. Sign in with Apple / Google Sign-In is optional. Account deletion is available in the app from Settings. Purchase restoration is available from the Premium screen.

Thank you for reconsidering the updated submission.
